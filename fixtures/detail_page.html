Description

GLPzy is a private GLP-1 shot and dose tracker for Zepbound, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Ozempic, semaglutide and tirzepatide routines.

Log doses, weight, symptoms, appetite, nutrition, progress photos and Apple Health context in one clean place. No in-app account required. Your records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

TRACK YOUR ROUTINE

• Log doses, dose dates, strengths, notes and injection sites
• Set reminders for weekly, daily, custom or compound routines
• Track weight, body measurements, symptoms, appetite and nutrition
• Record protein, water, calories, glucose context and cycle context where relevant
• Backfill prior history with manual entry or supported imports

SEE YOUR PROGRESS

• Review weight, BMI, consistency and trend charts
• Compare progress photos over time
• Browse calendar history and daily records
• View estimated level context for personal tracking only
• Add Apple Health read-only context where available

REVIEW WITH MORE CONTEXT

• Create a Provider Summary PDF for appointments or personal review
• Export your records as CSV, JSON or PDF
• Use widgets and Apple Watch for at-a-glance routine support
• Keep track of supplies, reminders and longer-term review tools with Premium

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

• No in-app account required
• Data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it
• Apple Health access is optional and read-only
• App Privacy label: Data Not Collected

PREMIUM

GLPzy Premium unlocks projected estimated level review, Provider Summary PDF, full-history exports, widgets, Apple Watch, supply tracking, maintenance and long-range review tools, advanced treatment controls and unlimited photo uploads.

IMPORTANT

GLPzy is for personal tracking only. It does not provide medical advice, dosing advice, treatment recommendations or diagnosis.

Estimated level context is not a measured blood concentration and must not be used for dosing decisions. Always speak with your clinician before making treatment decisions.

Medication names, availability, dosing forms and indications vary by country. GLPzy is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by medicine manufacturers.

What's new (v3.10)

GLPzy 3.10 brings a major reliability and tracking update.

• Imported doses now connect to the intended treatment, update Today and Estimated Exposure, show their source, and flag possible same-day duplicates.
• 3.5-day schedules stay saved and are used consistently. Compounded trackers can use a clearly labeled molecule-based estimate, and future planned doses no longer count as taken.
• Cycle entries now appear in Timeline and Calendar, with filters, editing, deletion, and improved past-date entry.
• Multi-use vial tracking now includes the amount remaining, vial selection while logging a dose, and a supply run-out estimate based on your current recorded schedule.
• Injection pain stays separate from injection-site reactions. Repeated same-day Apple Health weights are grouped into one history row without deleting source samples.
• Time formatting, graph comparison guidance, layouts, accessibility, and general stability have also been improved.
