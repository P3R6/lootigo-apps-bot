Frequently asked questions about free apps

How does the app stay in my library after it goes back to paid?

On the App Store, your purchase history is tied to your Apple ID. Once you "buy" the app at $0, it appears in your purchased list forever — you can reinstall it for free later, even if it has gone back to paid. This works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple ID.

Why do publishers make their apps free temporarily?

Several reasons: boost visibility in rankings (the App Store highlights apps that are trending), collect reviews, celebrate an app anniversary or event, or test price elasticity before a business model change. It's a win for you: grab the app for free and keep it forever.

How long does a "100% free" promo last?

On average 24 to 72 hours, sometimes less than 6 hours for flash sales. Rare cases exceed a week. That's why we recommend installing the app quickly as soon as you're interested, even if you don't plan to use it right away — the purchase is final in your Apple ID.

What about in-app purchases?

The base app stays free and yours forever, but in-app purchases (subscriptions, packs, unlocks) remain paid. Some freemium apps put their "Pro" full version on a $0 promo: this is usually flagged on the official App Store page. We never alter the price shown by Apple.
